How it works

1 · Candidates join

Scan the QR code or open the join link, pick an avatar and enter a name. No account, no app to install.

2 · Host runs the quiz

The host shows each question on screen (slides, images, anything), opens it in QuizApp to start the timer, and candidates submit A–D from their phones.

3 · Reveal and rank

Reveal the answer, see who got it and how fast, and follow the leaderboard through to the final podium.
